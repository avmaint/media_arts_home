Driving the ACME Tornado — five heads, forty pixels

00

What you're actually patching

The Tornado isn't one head — it's five, on a bar, each ringed by pixels. Understanding that structure is the whole job; the feature groups fall out of it.

First light — open the shutters

A freshly patched Tornado is dark even at full dimmer: both shutters default Closed and both dimmer masters default 0. There are two shutter stages — Shutter1 (beam) and Shutter2 (ring) — so opening "all shutters" means both. To get output:

Group "Tornado ALL" — or Fixture 301 Thru 305 Attribute "Shutter1" At "Open" — beam shutters, all bars Attribute "Shutter2" At "Open" — ring shutters, all bars At Full — intensity up (masters + cells)

At "Open" recalls the attribute's named Open channel set, so there's no DMX value to guess. Leave shutters open for normal use; the strobe/pulse ranges on the same two attributes are in Beam, and the intensity model is in Dimmer.

Five independent heads
  • Each head has its own pan & tilt (16-bit; pan swings 0–59°, tilt 0–221°), its own RGBL beam emitter, and its own motorised zoom. So the five heads aim, colour, and zoom separately.
  • RGBL = Red / Green / Blue / Lime. The lime emitter is a fourth mixing primary — it lifts yellow-greens and apparent brightness that RGB alone can't reach. In this GDTF it's the attribute ColorAdd_GY (green-yellow), alongside ColorAdd_R/G/B.
Five pixel rings
  • Each head is circled by a 24-LED ring. In 170-ch mode only, that ring is exposed as 8 RGB blocks (pixels) — so 5 heads × 8 blocks = 40 ring pixels, 120 channels of colour.
  • Smaller modes (71/75/85) collapse each ring to a single RGB + a macro-effect channel. You gave that up for per-pixel control by choosing 170.
How it lands in grandMA3
  • This GDTF (V1.50) resolves to 5 beam subfixtures + 40 ring-LED pixels (8 per head) on top of the base and yoke geometry — the 45 pixel/beam instances that make the Tornado explode into rows the moment you patch it.
  • That means subfixture selection and MAtricks are your primary tools, not the encoder alone. Colour phasers across the ring pixels are the fixture's signature look.
Profile this is written for · verified against the GDTF

This reference targets the patched GDTF ACME@TORNADO_(TB_5_IP)@V1.50.gdtf — the IP-rated five-head variant, library revision V1.50 — and the attribute names, pixel counts and channel layout below are read from that file. Beam colour is ColorAdd_R/G/B/GY; ring pixels are ColorAdd_R/G/B; each beam and each ring pixel also carries a virtual Dimmer (no DMX channel of its own), so grandMA3 gives you a real intensity encoder per sub-cell. Where this GDTF and the printed manual (Rev B) disagree — the global tail, §05 — the GDTF is what the console actually patches, so this document follows it. On a different library revision these specifics can shift; re-confirm in the sheet.

Where a control actually lives

The encoder bar shows up to five dual encoders at a time. A feature group with more than that spreads across several encoder pages, one per feature — the Encoder Page button to the left of the encoders names the current feature and shows how many pages the group has. Tap it to cycle, or tap and swipe for the list. Wherever a control on this fixture is not on the first page of its group, this document flags it later page.

Selecting & shaping the heads

Examples use fixture 301. A head is 301.2.H (yoke pan/tilt); its beam is .9, its ring pixels .1–.8. Heads are contiguous, so 301.2.1 Thru 5 is all five at once.

Uniform — every head aligned
Group "Tornado ALL" — or Fixture 301 Thru 305 Attribute "Pan" At 0 ; Attribute "Tilt" At 50

One value lands on every head identically.

Fan the heads (static splay)
Group "301 Heads" — one bar's 5 heads · = Fixture 301.2.1 Thru 5 Attribute "Tilt" At 30 Thru 70 — spreads head 1→5 across 30→70 Group "Heads ALL" ; Attribute "Tilt" At 20 Thru 80 — fan all 25 heads

The At x Thru y value-fan spreads across the selection in order. Reverse the numbers to tip it the other way; narrow the range for a gentler splay.

Shaped fans — set an Align mode, then adjust on the encoder
  • Align <> — peak at the centre (heads splay symmetrically out)
  • Align >< — trough at the centre (heads pinch in)
  • Align < / Align > — pivot on the first / last head
  • Align Off — back to uniform
Fanning vs. phasers vs. MAtricks

One idea, three levels: Align fans values once (a static spread); a phaser is a fan that moves (the effect engine runs the attribute between values over time); MAtricks decides how either spreads across the selection — the X / Y / Z phase, plus Wings (mirror from centre) and Blocks. On the Tornado: a tilt phaser on Heads ALL with X-phase is a wave running head-to-head, Y-phase rolls it bar-to-bar; a colour phaser on a ring-grid group (49–53) with X-phase chases around each ring, Y-phase steps head-to-head. The grid stored in each group is what MAtricks reads to tell "around the ring" from "along the rig." See the feature-group detail in §02, and the full command blocks in grandma3_utilities/snippets/movement_examples.md.

Dimmer

Preset Type 1

Two masters (beam / ring) over per-cell trim — and nothing lights until the shutter is open (see Beam).

Groups → scope
  • All bars: Group "Tornado ALL" · one bar: Group "Tornado 301" · beams-only / rings-only: the Beams / Rings groups · one head: Fixture 301.2.H.
How the level stacks
  • Beam master (ch166) and Ring master (ch168) are the overall levels, and both default to 0 — these are what you bring up for light. Independent: hold rings up while beams fade, or the reverse.
  • Per-cell dimmers (every beam + ring pixel) default to full — they're pixel-level trim and chases, scaling each cell under its master.
  • Output = shutter × master × per-cell × colour. Any one at zero = black.
Why it's dark at full

A fresh patch shows nothing even at full dimmer, because the shutters default Closed and the masters default 0. To light it: select the fixture, open the shutter (Beam), and bring up the master.

TORNADO · CH 166 (beam master) · CH 168 (ring master) Masters 8-bit; per-cell dimmers virtual 16-bit (default full). To raise overall level select a whole-fixture (Tornado) group; to trim one cell select that pixel subfixture.

Color

Preset Type 4

The heart of the fixture — two colour layers per head: the beam and the ring. This is where 128 of the 170 channels live.

The beam — RGBL, per head
  • Red Green Blue Lime mix the main beam of each head. Use the Colour Picker — with a lime emitter present, picking by hue/swatch beats dialling four raw faders.
  • Lime is a real fourth primary: it extends the gamut toward yellow-green and adds punch. In the picker it's handled for you; on the encoders it's its own attribute.
  • Groups: all beams Group "Beams ALL" · one bar Group "301 Beams" · one head's beam Fixture 301.2.H.9.
The ring — 8 RGB pixels per head
  • Each head's ring is 8 addressable RGB blocks (40 across the fixture). Select the ring pixels as subfixtures and drive them with MAtricks + colour phasers for chases, rainbows, and comet trails around each head.
  • Ring pixels are RGB only — no lime — so their gamut is slightly narrower than the beam's.
  • Groups: all rings Group "Rings ALL" · one bar Group "301 Rings" · one head's ring Fixture 301.2.H.1 Thru 8; chases use the grids 301 Grid / Rings Grid 8x25 / Rings Grid 40x5.
TORNADO · beam ch5–8 · ring ch10–33 (per head) Beam is ColorAdd_R/G/B/GY (ch5–8); the eight ring blocks are ColorAdd_R/G/B each (ch10–33), no GY. No CMY, no colour wheel, no CTO — it's an additive LED fixture, so colour is the emitters alone.

Beam

Preset Type 5

Two independent strobes — beam and ring — and the shutter that gates all output. No prism, gobo, frost, or iris on this fixture.

Groups → scope
  • Strobe follows its colour layer: beams via the Beams groups, rings via the Rings groups, both at once via the Tornado groups. Head / bar / all-bars as in the matrix above.
  • Beam Shutter (ch167, Shutter1) and Ring Shutter (ch169, Shutter2) — Closed, Open, strobe slow→fast, pulse (open/close), random. Push for the named ranges.
Shutter ↔ Dimmer

The shutter gates the dimmer: Closed = black regardless of dimmer level, Open passes the dimmed level, and strobe/pulse chop it on and off. Both shutters default Closed, so a fresh patch is dark until you open them — the usual "dimmer's up but nothing shows." Because beam and ring strobe separately, you can flash the rings while the beams hold steady, or the reverse.

TORNADO · CH 167 (beam) · CH 169 (ring) Global across their layer; no per-head strobe in this mode.

Focus

Preset Type 6

Five independent zooms — beam angle per head. Zoom lives on the head, so it uses the same selections as Position.

Groups → scope
  • All: Group "Heads ALL" · one bar: Group "301 Heads" · one head: Fixture 301.2.H. Fan with At x Thru y for a spread-to-tight sweep.
Attribute "Zoom" At 0 — wide Attribute "Zoom" At 100 — narrow / beam
TORNADO · per head · CH +9 of each head block Wide→Narrow (3–30° beam, 4–35° field). No separate focus attribute — it's a beam bar, not a spot.

Control

Preset Type 7

A single set-and-forget configuration channel. (This GDTF has no Pan/Tilt Speed channel — the printed manual lists one at 169, but V1.50 uses 169 for the ring strobe instead.)

  • Function (ch170) later page — a config channel: dimmer curves, fan mode, LED frequency, resets (pan/tilt, effect, all), head invert, dimmer speed, dimming start (high/low), Invert Pixel Order, and pan invert.
TORNADO · CH 170 The Function channel only acts when a value is held for a few seconds — it's for setup, not busking; keep it out of playback cues so a crossfade can't trigger a reset. Invert Pixel Order is the one worth knowing: it flips the ring-pixel numbering, which lets a single ring chase run the other way without re-patching.

Gobo & Shapers

not on this fixture

Both feature groups are empty for the Tornado — no wheels, no framing. If you tab to them on the encoder bar you'll find nothing to control. All the "effect" character comes from Colour (ring phasers) and Position (head movement) instead.

03

Programmer housekeeping

Two mechanics that save you from mystery states while building looks — identical to any grandMA3 fixture.

Activate vs. deactivate

Only active values store by default. A value can sit in the programmer inactive — present, affecting output, but skipped by Store/Update. Please Please activates everything for the selection; Off then a tap on a value knocks that one out; Off + a feature-group bank button releases the whole group.

The three states of Clear

Clear ×1 — deselect fixtures (values stay, still active) Clear ×2 — values stay but go deactivated (a store now = empty) Clear ×3 — programmer emptied, values released

Hold Clear for a second to jump straight to a full release.

Pixel work tip

With 40 ring pixels per fixture, it's easy to leave half a ring active and half not. Before storing a ring look, re-select the whole ring and Please Please so every pixel goes in together.

The short version

White on red is yours and will store. Red text on grey is yours but deactivated and won't. Everything else on a grey background is playback telling you where the level came from. On the Tornado, watch the shamrock GridPos layer in particular — it's doing the work on the ring pixels.

06

Selections & groups

How to reach the sub-cells by hand, and the standing group layout (30–64) built by the build_tornado_groups macro in the grandma3_utilities repo.

Sub-cell addressing (per fixture 30X, X = 1–5 → 301–305)
  • 30X.2.H — head H itself: yoke pan/tilt (H = 1–5). 30X.2.1 Thru 5 = all five heads.
  • 30X.2.H.9 — the beam of head H.
  • 30X.2.H.1 Thru 8 — that head's eight ring pixels.
Three syntax rules that bite
  • Repeat the keyword in a union: Fixture A + Fixture B, never Fixture A + B — a bare + B on a deep subfixture path throws illegal object.
  • Thru 8 is shorthand for the current level: Fixture 301.2.1.1 Thru 8 = ring pixels 1–8 of head 1.
  • Grid cells first, then select: Grid 1 Thru 8 / n sets the destination row; the next selection fills it in order, and each step accumulates into the grid. This is how a 2-D pixel grid is built — a plain union is only ever one row.

The macro deletes 30–70 and rebuilds, so it is safe to re-run. Grid groups keep their arrangement, so a MAtricks phaser recalled through them runs along the intended axis.
