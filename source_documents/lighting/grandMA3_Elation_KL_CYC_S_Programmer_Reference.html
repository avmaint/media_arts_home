Driving the Elation KL CYC S — two segments, five emitters, one dimmer

A working reference for the KL CYC S in 32-channel Extended 2 Zone mode: the fullest personality the fixture offers — two independent RGBMA colour segments at 16-bit, a variable CCT engine per segment, green/magenta shift per segment, a gel swatch book, and the EdgeLight safety glow. One shared dimmer sits over all of it.

00

What you're actually patching

A 90 W asymmetric cyc light with a five-colour array, split into two control segments in this mode. It doesn't move; the programming is colour, level, and a handful of engines that shape white.

RGBMA — five emitters, not four
  • Red · Green · Blue · Mint · Amber. Mint is the unusual one: it fills the cyan-green gap that makes RGB whites look thin, and it's why this fixture reads CRI 96.5 / TLCI 88 rather than the usual LED numbers.
  • Every emitter is a coarse/fine pair in this mode — 16-bit across the board, including the dimmer.
  • 5,645 lumens, asymmetric optics: 25° × 75° bare, 40° × 90° with the included frost. Built to wash a cyc from close range, or to sit on the deck as a footlight.
Two segments — the "2 Zone" part
  • The array splits into two control segments, numbered 1 and 2 in Elation's pixel-grouping diagram. Each gets its own five emitters, its own variable CCT, and its own green shift.
  • What they do not get is their own intensity. The dimmer on channels 1–2 is global — see the callout in the Dimmer group.
  • Confirm which half of the fixture is segment 1 by bumping one segment up on a bench unit. The diagram is in the manual, but it's quicker to look.
How it lands in grandMA3
  • Expect two subfixtures, one per segment, each with five colour attributes plus CCT and green shift; the dimmer, strobe, swatch book, EdgeLight and the two config channels sit on the main fixture.
  • The Colour Picker will reach R/G/B. Mint and Amber are additive attributes you set on encoders or bake into colour presets — a picker-only workflow leaves two-fifths of the array at zero.
  • Attribute spellings vary by GDTF: Mint may appear as Mint, ColorAdd_M or similar, and the CCT engine as CTC or a named colour-control attribute. Push an encoder to read the live name before scripting.
EdgeLight glows when you're at zero

EdgeLight is a safety feature: with the main output at 0%, the fixture can hold a dim red or blue glow to mark the edge of the deck. That is exactly the behaviour you don't want in a theatrical blackout if nobody told you about it. It's driven by channel 30 in this mode, but the fixture's menu can also set it to Always On, in which case channel 30 stops having any say and the colour and level come from the menu defaults. If a "dark" cyc isn't dark, this is why.

Verify on your profile

Whether the two segments import as subfixtures or as flat attributes depends on the GDTF/library revision. Patch one, expand it in the fixture sheet, and read the structure before scripting. The fixture also speaks sACN/Art-Net over its RJ45 ports and supports RDM — worth knowing which path a given unit is actually listening on.

Where a control actually lives

The encoder bar shows up to five dual encoders at a time. A feature group with more than that spreads across several encoder pages, one per feature — the Encoder Page button to the left of the encoders names the current feature and shows how many pages the group has. Tap it to cycle, or tap and swipe for the list. Wherever a control on this fixture is not on the first page of its group, this document flags it later page.

Selecting segments

Fixture 70 — the whole unit (both segments) Fixture 70.1 Thru 70.2 — the two segments, as subfixtures At 50 — the shared dimmer, whichever segment you selected

Two cells is not much of a pixel map, but it's enough for a warm/cool split across a cyc, or a colour break between the top and bottom of a wash. MAtricks works, it just has two cells to play with.

02

Feature group by feature group

Four groups carry this fixture: Dimmer, Colour, Beam and Control. Position, Gobo, Focus and Shapers are empty — there are no optics to move.

Dimmer

Preset Type 1

One 16-bit intensity for the whole fixture, plus the curve and delay behaviour.

  • Dimmer (ch 1–2) — coarse and fine. At 50, Full, Off as usual.
  • Dim Modes (ch 31) later page — Standard, Stage, TV, Architectural, Theatre, Stage 2, then a bank of fixed dimmer delay times from 0 s to 10 s. A snap channel.
  • Control (ch 32) also carries the dimmer curves — Linear, Square, Inverse Square, S-Curve — up at values 201–240.
The dimmer is global, the colour isn't

Both segments share channels 1–2. There is no per-segment intensity anywhere in this mode, so a level difference between the two halves has to come from their emitter values — you dim segment 2 by scaling its RGBMA, not by dimming it. Console-side that means executor faders and intensity masters move the whole fixture together, and any segment balance you build lives inside your colour values. Store it in colour presets and it'll travel; expect it to fight you if you try to fade one segment out under a master.

KL CYC S · CH 1–2 · 31 · 32 16-bit dimming with selectable curves is the reason to run an extended personality on a cyc wash — the bottom 5% is where a cyc lives, and Linear vs. S-Curve visibly changes how a slow fade lands there.

Color

Preset Type 4

Two segments of RGBMA at 16-bit, two CCT engines, two green shifts, and a swatch book over the top.

Per-segment mixing — Red Green Blue Mint Amber
  • Five emitters fill one encoder page exactly, so a segment's whole mix is on the first page. Segment 1 on channels 4–13, segment 2 on 14–23, each emitter a coarse/fine pair in R·G·B·Mint·Amber order.
  • Mint and Amber do the work in tints and near-whites. A saturated blue barely needs them; a warm straw or a clean skin wash will look wrong without them.
White — Variable CCT (ch 24) and Variable CCT 2 (ch 25) later page
  • 0–23 = Open (the CCT engine stands down), 24–85 sweeps 2400 K to 8500 K, 86–255 holds 8500 K. One per segment, so you can run a warm bottom and a cool top.
  • CCT Cross Fade (ch 26) picks which engine you hear: 0 = CCT, 255 = Color Mix. The published chart lists 1–254 as "no function," so treat it as a two-position switch rather than a fade, and verify on a bench unit if you were hoping to crossfade between a mixed colour and a calibrated white.
Shaping and shortcuts
  • Green Shift (ch 28) and Green Shift 2 (ch 29) later page — 0 idle, 1–127 full minus-green through neutral, 128 neutral white, 129–255 neutral through full plus-green. Per segment. This is your plus/minus green correction for camera or for matching a neighbouring fixture.
  • Color (ch 27) later page — a virtual gel swatch book at 1–179, then scroll modes: clockwise 180–201, stop 202–207, counter-clockwise 208–229, random slots 235–249. 0 leaves it open so your own mix shows through.
Three ways to make a colour

Emitter mix, CCT engine and swatch book all drive the same LEDs, and a value left behind in any of them will argue with the other two. The habit that keeps this fixture predictable: pick one system per look, and store an explicit zero in the other two — CCT Open, Colour open — so nothing arrives by tracking from an earlier cue. If a mix won't take, check channels 24–27 before you check your maths.

KL CYC S · CH 4–23 · 24–27 · 28–29 Build two families of colour presets — mixes and whites — and keep them apart, exactly as you would on the Cylinder One Spectrum. Both fixtures punish a half-stored colour in the same way.

Beam

Preset Type 5

One shutter/strobe channel, covering the whole fixture.

  • Shutter / Strobe (ch 3) — 0–31 closed, 64–95 strobe slow to fast, 128–159 pulse sequences, 192–223 random strobe. The gaps between those bands (32–63, 96–127, 160–191, 224–255) are all "shutter open."
  • The default value is 50 — inside an open band — so an unprogrammed fixture sits open rather than dark.
KL CYC S · CH 3 Strobe is global: both segments flash together. There's no prism, gobo, frost or iris — the frost is a physical filter that ships in the box, not a channel.

Control

Preset Type 7

EdgeLight, dimmer behaviour, refresh rate — the settings channels.

  • EdgeLight Level (ch 30) later page — 0–10 off, 11–20 default, 21–255 minimum to maximum intensity for the edge glow.
  • Control (ch 32) later page — EdgeLight behaviour (disabled / always on / DMX / red / blue) at 70–99, LED refresh rate from 900 Hz to 25 kHz at 100–168, and the dimmer curves at 201–240. A snap channel.
  • Dim Modes (ch 31) — listed under Dimmer above; also a snap channel.
Keep 31 and 32 out of playback

Both are snap channels carrying configuration, not looks. A cue that fades through them will park on intermediate values on the way past — and those values mean "change the refresh rate" or "switch the dimmer curve." Set them once, park them, or store them deliberately in a single preset-state cue; don't let them ride along in a crossfading sequence.

KL CYC S · CH 30–32 Refresh rate matters if these ever appear on camera: the high settings (10 kHz and up) are there for exactly that. It's a fixture-level decision, so make it once on the unit or in a single dedicated cue rather than per-look.

Position · Gobo · Focus · Shapers

not on this fixture

All four are empty. The KL CYC S is a fixed asymmetric wash — its only optical variable is which physical filter you slide into the frame, and the barndoor you point with your hands.

03

Programmer housekeeping

Two mechanics that save you from mystery states while building looks — identical to any grandMA3 fixture.

Activate vs. deactivate

Only active values store by default. A value can sit in the programmer inactive — present, affecting output, but skipped by Store/Update. Please Please activates everything for the selection; Off then a tap on a value knocks that one out; Off + a feature-group bank button releases the whole group.

The three states of Clear

Clear ×1 — deselect fixtures (values stay, still active) Clear ×2 — values stay but go deactivated (a store now = empty) Clear ×3 — programmer emptied, values released

Hold Clear for a second to jump straight to a full release.

Store both segments

It's easy to build a look on segment 1 and leave segment 2 tracking from an earlier cue. Re-select the whole fixture and Please Please before storing, so both halves — and the CCT, shift and swatch channels that go with them — land in the cue together.

The short version

White on red is yours and will store. Red text on grey is yours but deactivated and won't. Everything else on a grey background is playback telling you where the level came from. On this fixture, a colour that won't take usually means a tracked value in CCT (24–25) or the swatch book (27) — look there before you look at your mix.
